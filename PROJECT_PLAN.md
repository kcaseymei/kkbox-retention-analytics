# KKBox Retention Analytics — Project Plan

## Business Problem

Subscription businesses need to identify customers at risk of churn early enough

to take meaningful retention actions.

This project analyzes KKBox subscription, transaction, member, and listening

behavior data to understand which behaviors are associated with churn and when

retention interventions may be most useful.

## Primary Question

What subscriber behaviors and transaction patterns are associated with churn,

and how can KKBox use those signals to improve retention?

## Analytical Questions

1. What does churn look like across the subscriber base?

2. Which transaction and subscription behaviors are associated with churn?

3. How does engagement differ between retained and churned subscribers?

4. When do behavioral signals of churn begin to appear?

5. Which subscriber segments show elevated churn risk?

6. How could these findings inform retention strategies?

## Planned Workflow

1. Data audit and quality assessment

2. Define churn and analytical population

3. Build analytical data model

4. Exploratory and cohort analysis

5. Transaction and subscription behavior analysis

6. Listening engagement analysis

7. Churn driver analysis

8. Predictive modeling

9. Model interpretation and validation

10. Retention recommendations
