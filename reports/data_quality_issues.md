# Data quality issue and treatment register

Date: 2026-10-05

Scope: every item is a **full-data** finding from `notebooks/01_data_audit.ipynb` (section in brackets), on both releases unless stated. Severity is a reviewer judgement of the risk to later analysis, not a measure of how many rows are affected. **Treatments are planned; none has been applied.** No source record has been removed, imputed, or modified.
