2 · Targeting

How many churners does a short contact list catch?

Each month every expiring user gets a risk score, using only data known on the last day of the previous month. The team contacts the top of the list. "Share of churners reached" is the share of that month's churners who are on the list.

Decision it supports: Use the risk score or a simple rule, and how early to score before expiry.

5 · Drivers

What the score relies on

The subscription record (renewal mode, payment, transaction history) carries most of the signal. Listening behavior adds a little on top.

Decision it supports: Which data to keep collecting and which message fits which user.

Listening change and churn

Listening days in the last 30 days compared with the 30 days before. "Down" and "Up" mean a change of more than 25%. Test months 2017-01 and 2017-02.

Auto-renew on

Manual renewers

Users who stop or cut back listening churn more in both groups. The two panels use different scales: manual renewers churn about ten times as often.

Which inputs the score depends on most

Each input is scrambled in turn and the model is re-scored. A longer bar means the model ranks churners worse without that input (drop in AUC, January 2017).

Payment method matters most, but the dataset does not say what each code means, so no business reading is drawn from it. Related inputs share credit, so a short bar does not mean an input is useless.

6 · Actions and test plan

Three actions to test, in priority order

These are hypotheses. A high risk score shows who is likely to leave, not who can be persuaded to stay; only a randomized test shows that.

Decision it supports: What to launch, to whom, and the rule for rolling it out or stopping it.

What this analysis does not show

  • No proven retention gain. Nothing here shows that contacting users keeps them. That needs the experiment above.
  • Results are weaker on the official March 2017 labels. The official file treats early renewers differently, and 11.2% of its users are outside the population the model was built for.
  • Labels are reliable only up to 2017-02. Renewals after 2017-03-31 are not in the data, so later months cannot be labeled.
  • Profile data may look ahead. The member table is a 2017-11 snapshot, so some profile values may postdate the scoring date.
